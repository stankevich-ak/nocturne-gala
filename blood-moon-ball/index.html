Rehearsals

Learning the Dances

Before the Blood Moon Ball, guests came together for a guided dance workshop at Quadra Village Community Centre on October 24. We learned the steps, practised partner changes and formations, and met the people we would share the dance floor with.

From first waltz steps to lively group dances, the afternoon helped beginners arrive at the Ball with familiar faces and a little more confidence.

  • Basic waltz
  • Figure waltz
  • Menuet
  • Gothic dance
  • Polka for Three
  • Slow menuet
  • Cotillion
  • Chapelloise
The Ball

A Night Under the Blood Moon

Vampire aesthetics, dramatic costumes, and candlelight transformed the evening into a gothic gathering. Guests waltzed, joined group dances, and made the ballroom their own.

Live music, performances, fun ballroom games, and plenty of shared laughter filled the night. Between dances, there was time to meet new people, admire the outfits, and enjoy the atmosphere together.
