End-of-Summer Historic Dance Picnic

Port Moody, BC · September 11, 2026

A free community event · potluck picnic with beginner-friendly historic dancing, games, food, and good company.

View RecapPlaylist

How it was

About 30 people joined us for an evening of dancing and good company. People brought food to share, and we had a lovely time eating, socializing, and learning five dances together:

  • La Chasse
  • Minuet
  • Spring Bolt
  • Circassian Circle
  • Waterloo Promenade
