End-of-Summer Historic Dance Picnic

Friday, September 11 · 6:00 PM · Rocky Point Park, Port Moody

Join us for a free potluck picnic with beginner-friendly historic dancing, games, food, and good company.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear?
Think cute end-of-summer picnic: dresses, skirts, linen shirts, vests, vintage-inspired outfits, or simply something comfortable to dance in. Historic clothing is welcome, but absolutely not required.
What should I bring?
Bring a picnic blanket and something to share. Food, snacks, drinks, cards, lawn games, acoustic instruments, or anything else suited to a relaxed park evening are all welcome.
What dances are we going to learn?
La Chapelloise, Minuet, Circassian Circle, and Waterloo Promenade. We’ll teach everything there, so complete beginners are welcome.
What time do we meet?
We meet at 6:00 PM on Friday, September 11, 2026.
Where do we meet?
At Rocky Point Park in Port Moody, next to the stage.
Are there any other events?
Yes. Nocturne Gala is also organizing the Celestial Winter Masquerade in Vancouver this winter.
