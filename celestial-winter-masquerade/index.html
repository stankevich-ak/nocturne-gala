The Experience

You’ve seen evenings like this in films and period dramas: candlelit rooms, elegant music, beautiful clothes, and people moving together like they already belong there.

Nocturne Gala makes that feeling possible for real guests. Before the ball, we teach beginner-friendly adaptations of historic dances through guided rehearsals and workshops, so you arrive knowing what to do, who you’re dancing with, and how to take part.

When Vancouver turns dark early, the ballroom begins to glow

On a winter night in Vancouver, the city quiets before the evening has even begun. Streetlights reflect on wet pavement, windows glow, and guests arrive at the Scottish Cultural Centre behind masks, velvet, satin, and candlelit anticipation.

The Masquerade begins before December 18. In rehearsals, strangers become familiar faces. You learn the steps, find the rhythm, and arrive at the Ball already carrying part of the story with you.

The mask is not only decoration. It gives you permission to leave the ordinary version of yourself at the door: the daily routine, the awkward first introductions, the fear of not knowing what to do.

By the time the music starts, the room is no longer anonymous. It is Vancouver in winter, transformed for one evening into a place of movement, ceremony, and dancing beneath the stars.

For one evening, the city feels farther away. The ballroom feels closer to a dream.

Your Journey

Learn together. Connect early. Arrive ready. The rehearsals are part of the experience. You’ll meet people, learn the dances step by step, and walk into the Ball already feeling at home.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I Need Dance Experience?
No. The rehearsals are beginner-friendly and teach the dances step by step. The event is designed so first-time guests can participate without feeling lost.
Are Rehearsals Included in My Ticket?
Yes. Your ticket includes four pre-event rehearsals (2 hours each). They are a central part of the experience and help guests learn the dances, meet others, and arrive prepared. Rehearsals take place at Lochdale Community School, 6990 Aubrey Street, Burnaby, BC V5B 2E5.
Do I Need to Bring a Partner?
Depending on your ticket type, you can come alone (Leader or Follower ticket) or with a partner (Couples ticket). See ticket options →
What Should I Wear?
Elegant formalwear is expected. Masquerade, celestial, winter, historical, or romantic details are encouraged — provided the outfit remains polished and event-appropriate. See the Costume Inspiration Board →
What Is Included with My Ticket?
Entry to the Ball, four rehearsals, a guided program, winter decor, performers and live music, professional photo/video coverage, light snacks, and drinks. Full list →
Is There an Age Restriction?
This event is for guests aged 14 and older. Guests under 18 must be accompanied by an adult.
What Is the Refund Policy?
All sales are final, but ticket transfers are allowed before the start of rehearsals.
