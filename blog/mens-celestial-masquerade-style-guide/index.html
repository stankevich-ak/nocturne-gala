What to Wear to the Celestial Winter Masquerade: Gentlemen

Formalwear, celestial details, statement shirts, waistcoats, and masks for an elegant winter masquerade outfit.

Gentlemen, your invitation has arrived. Now comes the question: what does one wear to a celestial winter masquerade?

You can start with a polished formal outfit and add celestial details, or build a more dramatic look with a waistcoat, tailcoat, or statement shirt. You don’t need a complete costume or strict historical accuracy. Aim for an outfit that feels elegant, fits the winter masquerade, and lets you move comfortably while dancing.

The Celestial Winter Masquerade takes place in Vancouver on December 18, 2026. A costume and mask are required. Start with elegant formalwear and add celestial, winter, historical, or romantic details. See the event details.

The Basics

Begin with a dress shirt, dress pants, and formal shoes. No jeans, sneakers, or shorts. Gloves are highly recommended, and a mask is mandatory. A tuxedo, jacket, waistcoat, or tailcoat is optional.

Palette

Choose black, blue, dark purple, gold, ivory, white, or silver. A dark base with an ivory shirt or a touch of gold or silver makes an easy celestial combination. Velvet, satin, jacquard, brocade, lace, and embroidery add texture; a star-patterned shirt or a metallic accessory can bring in a little winter shimmer.

Where to Find a Men’s Masquerade Outfit

Here are some places to look, grouped by approximate budget.

Budget symbols are approximate. Check current prices, garment measurements, availability, and delivery dates before ordering.

1. Facebook Marketplace and Thrift Stores: $

Look for dress shirts, formal trousers, velvet jackets, waistcoats, and tailcoats on Facebook Marketplace or in local thrift stores. Try search terms like “brocade vest,” “velvet blazer,” “jacquard waistcoat,” or “men’s formalwear.” Look beyond the costume section: one distinctive secondhand piece can be enough to build an outfit around.

2. SHEIN: $–$$

SHEIN has affordable ruffled shirts, patterned pieces, and waistcoats that can make a simple outfit feel more theatrical. Consider this blue floral ruffled shirt, a gothic jacquard shirt, or this black floral jacquard waistcoat. Check the garment measurements before ordering.

3. Amazon: $–$$

Amazon has waistcoats and other statement pieces that can be paired with a shirt and dress trousers. This steampunk-style waistcoat could work with a simple ivory or black shirt.

4. Fashionable Formalwear: $$

For a modern look you can wear again, start with tailored clothing and add a statement shirt or jacket.

  • Fashion Nova: The Star Gazer embellished jacket pairs well with a black or ivory shirt, formal trousers, and a silver accessory.
  • Charles Tyrwhitt: Try a navy floral shirt for a little pattern, or a white twill shirt as a simple base beneath a waistcoat or jacket.
  • Orttu: Explore distinctive shirts and jackets for a more expressive silhouette.

5. Dramatic Costumes: $$

For a more theatrical look, explore ruffled shirts, textured waistcoats, and long coats from these shops:

  • DevilInspired: Browse a gothic shirt, an asymmetrical tailcoat vest, or a gothic dress coat.
  • HARD’N’HEAVY: Try a Victorian jacquard waistcoat or a white shirt with a black chiffon tie and stand collar.
  • GothNRock: Look at a gothic tailcoat for a longer silhouette.
  • Devil Fashion: The Superstition gothic dress coat adds lace detailing and an asymmetrical hem.

6. Historical Clothing: $$

For fuller sleeves, high collars, and a historical silhouette, browse men’s shirts at Historical Emporium. Pair a shirt with dress trousers and a waistcoat, then add a cravat or brooch to finish the look.

Masks

A mask is mandatory. Choose one that feels secure, lets you see clearly, and stays comfortable while you dance. Try a Venetian-style half-face mask or a classic black eye mask from SHEIN. You can also decorate your own to match your outfit.

The easiest place to start is with one piece you love, then build the rest of your outfit around it. We look forward to seeing what you wear beneath the winter lights.
