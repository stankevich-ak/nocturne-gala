What to Wear to the Blood Moon Ball: Men’s Vampire Costume Guide

Affordable costumes, gothic shirts, waistcoats, jackets, accessories, and local rentals for an elegant vampire outfit you can comfortably dance in.

Gentlemen, your invitation has arrived. Now comes the question: what does one wear to a vampire ball?

You can take one of two approaches:

  1. Find a complete costume. The simplest option when you want everything coordinated and ready to wear.
  2. Build your own character. Begin with clothing you already own, then add a distinctive shirt, velvet waistcoat, tailcoat, jewellery, or other gothic details.

Your outfit does not have to be historically perfect or terribly expensive. It should look intentional, feel appropriate for an elegant vampire ball, and allow you to dance comfortably. For visual inspiration, explore our Blood Moon Ball costume inspiration board.

Begin with affordable options

Second-hand marketplaces and thrift stores

Search for complete vampire, gothic, Victorian, or steampunk costumes. For individual pieces, look for a poet shirt, velvet waistcoat, brocade vest, tailcoat, frock coat, or formal black jacket. Retired theatre and Halloween costumes can be excellent value.

Value Village and other thrift stores bring out Halloween collections in early autumn. A velvet jacket or ruffled shirt can become suitably immortal with the right styling.

Pieces you can wear again

The Star Gazer embellished black jacket works with a black or cream shirt, fitted trousers, gloves, and a ruby-coloured accessory. For historical silhouettes, browse Renaissance Wardrobe and Historical Emporium.

A white or cream shirt is often more effective than an entirely black outfit because the contrast shows your silhouette and accessories in photographs.

More distinctive options

HARD’N’HEAVY carries gothic, Victorian, and steampunk shirts with jacquard fabrics, ruffled collars, lace jabots, embroidery, and detachable collars. For a romantic ancient-vampire look, consider HolyClothing’s Trystan vest or Orion shirt.

Etsy is a stronger fit for unusual, handmade, or made-to-measure pieces. This Victorian red tapestry vest could become the centrepiece of a Blood Moon Ball costume.

Rent a costume in Victoria

You can rent a complete costume instead of purchasing one. Ask Disguise the Limit Costume Rentals or Langham Court Theatre Costume Loft about vampire, Victorian, gothic, aristocratic, and historical men’s costumes, including frock coats, tailcoats, waistcoats, and capes.

Choose fabrics and colours that photograph well

Look for velvet or velour, brocade and jacquard, satin, lace, embroidery, natural linen or cotton, and dark metallic details. Black is the easiest foundation, but an entirely black outfit can disappear in evening photographs.

Add burgundy, oxblood, deep red, cream, antique gold, silver, forest green, or dark teal. A cream shirt beneath a black waistcoat will usually photograph better than a black shirt beneath a black jacket.

Make sure you can dance in it

The Blood Moon Ball is an immersive Halloween event with historic social dancing. Avoid clothing that is extremely heavy or restricts your shoulders, arms, or legs.

  • Very long capes and coat tails
  • Loose chains
  • Heavy layers
  • Tight shoulders or restrictive trousers
  • Slippery or uncomfortable shoes

Try on the full outfit before the pre-ball dance workshop. Walk, sit, turn, and lift your arms in it.

Wear gloves

Gloves complete the formal silhouette and make hand contact during partner dancing feel softer and more comfortable. Simple black, cream, white, grey, or burgundy dress gloves are enough.

If you cannot find a pair, we will bring a small selection to the pre-ball dance workshop on October 24, available for purchase while supplies last.

Finish the character with accessories

Accessories transform formal clothing into a character. Think roses, blood, rubies, lace, silver, antique gold, and objects that appear to carry a family curse.

  • A cravat or neck scarf and decorative pin
  • A rose, ruby, or cameo brooch
  • A signet ring, chain, or pocket watch
  • One dramatic earring or pendant
  • A dark red pocket square

Jewellery is entirely appropriate for a gentleman at a vampire ball. A little decadence is encouraged. We will also bring a small collection of accessories for purchase at the October 24 workshop.

Your invitation awaits

The Blood Moon Ball takes place Friday, October 30, 2026, at Esquimalt Gorge Pavilion in Victoria. It is a beginner-friendly Halloween ball with historic dancing, gothic music, candlelight, costumes, and a four-hour dance workshop included with your ticket.

No previous dance experience is required. You simply need a costume, a willingness to learn, and perhaps a convincing explanation for why you have not aged since 1897.
